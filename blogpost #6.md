# Punch - Final Blog Post & Project Conclusion

## What is Punch?

Punch is a third-person survival game set inside a zoological garden. You play as a small monkey named Punch, who is just trying to mind his own business, until the other monkeys in the zoo decide to start chasing and fighting him.

Your goal is simple: survive, collect stones from the ground, and throw them at the enemy monkeys to take them out. Every kill earns you points, and your final score is your total kills multiplied by 100. How long can you last, and how high can you score?

---

## Gameplay

The core gameplay loop is straightforward but fast paced. Enemy monkeys continuously spawn into the arena and immediately start chasing Punch. Stones are scattered across the ground, walk over one to automatically pick it up, then aim and throw it at an incoming enemy to take them down.

The challenge comes from managing multiple enemies at once while keeping track of where the next stone is. As enemies pile up, the pressure increases and every decision counts.

To help you survive, Teddybears occasionally appear on the ground. Picking one up activates a blue protective bubble around Punch for 7 seconds, giving you a brief window of safety to reposition and plan your next move.

### The loop in short

1. Enemy monkeys spawn and chase Punch
2. Walk over stones to pick them up
3. Throw stones at enemies to eliminate them
4. Pick up Teddybears for temporary protection
5. Survive as long as possible and maximize your kill score

## Development Journey

Punch was developed across three milestones, each building on the last.

In **Milestone 1** we built the foundation: a basic playable prototype with enemy AI, stone pickup and throwing mechanics, and a simple arena to test everything in. The core gameplay loop was in place, but the game was still very rough visually and mechanically.

In **Milestone 2** we expanded significantly. We switched to a first-person camera, introduced the Teddybear shield power-up, added a health and damage system, updated the enemy model with walking, hitting, and death animations, and built out the gameplay UI with a live health and score display.

By **Milestone 3** the game came together as a complete experience — with a main menu, death screen, pause screen, sound effects and full animations.

---

## Conclusion

Punch started as a simple idea: a small monkey in a zoo, trying to survive against the odds. Over the course of development it grew from a rough prototype into a fully playable game with a clear identity and a satisfying gameplay loop.

The project taught us a lot about building interconnected game systems, iterating on mechanics based on how the game actually feels to play, and the importance of getting a core loop working early so everything else can be built on top of it.

We are proud of what Punch became. It is fast, fun, and does exactly what it sets out to do put you in the shoes of a scrappy little monkey and see how long you can last.

Thanks for following the development of *Punch*.
